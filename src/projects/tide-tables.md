## Context

The club decided when to launch by reading a printed tide table and doing the
arithmetic in their heads. Mistakes meant dragging boats over mud.

## The chart

A week of tides as one continuous curve, with the club's safe launch window
shaded on top. Hovering — or tapping on a phone — shows the exact height and
time. The whole thing is a single SVG generated at build time from public tide
predictions, so it loads instantly at the slipway on one bar of signal.

## Since

The chart is now the first thing on the club's home page, and the launch
window is the number members quote to each other.
